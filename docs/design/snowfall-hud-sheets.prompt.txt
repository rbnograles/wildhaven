==================== SHEET 1: PANELS ====================

Use the attached picture as the exact style and design reference. It is the in-game HUD concept for Wildhaven, a cozy hand-painted top-down survival game, in its snowfall winter look. Make a UI COMPONENT SHEET: the HUD panels from that picture pulled apart and laid out one by one on a single image, so each can be cut out and used in the game.

Canvas: landscape, 1536 x 1024.
Background: one flat, solid, pure magenta #FF00FF over the whole canvas. No gradient, texture, shadow, vignette, scenery or falling snow on the background. Do not use magenta or pink anywhere in the artwork itself.
Layout: tidy rows, at least 48 px of empty magenta between any two pieces and from every canvas edge. Nothing overlaps or touches. No drop shadows spilling onto the background. Every piece fully visible, front-facing and flat (no perspective tilt), lit softly from the upper left.
No text, letters, numbers, labels, watermarks or grid lines anywhere.

Draw these 8 panels, all EMPTY inside (no text, icons, items or scenery):
1. Goals card: parchment panel in a snowy weathered wood frame with bronze corner rivets, snow piled on the top edge, small icicles under the bottom edge.
2. Region name banner: wide wooden plaque with a moss-green felt centre strip and a small snowy pine tree emblem at each end, snow on top. Centre left blank.
3. Player card panel: wide, low wooden frame with stitched moss-green felt inside, snow on top, icicles below. Plain empty interior.
4. Item box panel: wide wooden frame with stitched moss-green felt inside, a small green felt tab with dashed stitching at the top centre, snow on top, icicles below. Plain empty interior.
5. Round minimap frame: thick round carved wood ring with bronze studs and a small blank compass plate at the top, snow on top, icicles at the bottom. The inside of the ring is solid magenta.
6. Toast strip: short, wide parchment strip in a snowy wood frame, with a small round empty icon socket at its left end.
7. Build menu popup: parchment card in a snowy wood frame with a small downward-pointing tail centred on the bottom edge.
8. Key prompt pill: small dark-wood rounded pill with an empty light key-cap square on its left.

Keep each frame's sides even and straight so the panels can be stretched (9-slice). Snow caps and icicles stay attached to their own frame.

Style: match the reference exactly. Warm hand-painted 2D indie RPG UI, weathered carved wood with bronze corner rivets, stitched moss-green felt with dashed stitching, torn parchment, soft clean dark brown outlines. Snowfall look: soft fresh snow piled on top edges, small icicles under bottom edges, light frost in the wood grain, pale ice-blue and snow-white highlights balanced with warm wood, pine green and ochre. Cozy, not cold.
