Wildhaven world

    Place a sprite

    Pick a ground, then click or drag on the map to paint boxes. The Eraser turns boxes to sea. With no ground picked, dragging moves the map; holding Space does too. A painted box shows its new ground at once; the soft edges, the shore banks and the trees and grass that belong on it are made when you rebuild.

    Hills

    Raise and Lower work on blocks of 2 by 2 boxes, one level at a time. Stairs: click the south edge or the wall of a hill to add a flight, or an existing flight to remove it. The walls and edges of the hill are drawn when you rebuild.

    Problems

    Places or objects changed since the map was last built, so the position warnings may be out of date. Rebuild for the real answer.
